Local preparation
Geometry, skin weights, skeletons, morph targets and license metadata unchanged.
Textures capped at 1024px; embedded thumbnails reduced to 256px. Opaque diffuse
images use JPEG quality 90, without chroma subsampling. Alpha and normal maps
remain lossless PNG after any resizing. Small palettes are lossless, exact-color
palettes. Texture/image indices are unchanged. No animation clips are embedded.
