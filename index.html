how the distances work

Visible distance compares the cells each viewpoint can see after washers, dryers and furniture block the rays.

Attention distance measures the effort needed to move one attention pattern onto another across the room.

The attention patterns are written for this simulation. They are not eye tracking.

Proximity rules use body distance, machine clearance, exit clearance and shared attention. The plan uses an approximate scale of 1.8 centimeters per drawing unit.

Position recognizes between, adjacent and among. Movement uses start and end distances for toward and away, and the whole recorded path for past. Click an open tile to walk around furniture.

The room is a stylized cutaway. Sight is computed on its ground plane with solid furniture footprints. It does not model over-the-table vision or human cognition.
